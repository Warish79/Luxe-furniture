About Us

Mumbai's Trusted Luxury Furniture Showroom

Welcome to Luxe Furniture, a premium furniture showroom located in Oshiwara, Jogeshwari West, Mumbai. Founded by Mr Saif Khan, we specialise in crafting high-quality furniture for homes, offices, hotels, restaurants, cafés, villas, and commercial spaces.

Every piece we create reflects our commitment to premium craftsmanship, superior materials, and timeless design. Whether you need a single statement piece or a complete interior solution, we deliver excellence.
